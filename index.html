Case 01 — Campaign Strategy · Experiential

The Sōlace Experience

A full campaign concept repositioning a premium boat builder as the entry point to modern boating culture. The idea: partner with prestigious yacht clubs worldwide on a curated membership package — boat access, rentals, F&B, and on-water experiences — so 20–40 year-olds can buy into the lifestyle before they buy the boat.

I developed the strategy end to end: objective, audience definition, tone of voice, brand look & feel, deliverables, and partnership mandatories — anchored by the line "Quality on the water. Powered by SŌLACE."
